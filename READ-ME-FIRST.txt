YOUR WEBSITE + ADMIN PANEL  -  SIMPLE GUIDE
============================================

LOGIN FOR TESTING (change it right away - see Step 3)
   Address : yourwebsite.com/admin
   Username: admin
   Password: Admin@123


STEP 1 - UPLOAD THE FILES TO YOUR HOSTING
-----------------------------------------
Your hosting must support PHP (almost every hosting does - ask your host if unsure).

A) Using your hosting control panel (cPanel / hPanel) - easiest
   1. Log in to your hosting account and open "File Manager".
   2. Open the folder called  public_html  (this is your website's main folder.
      On some hosts it is called "www" or "htdocs").
   3. Click "Upload" and choose the ZIP file you downloaded.
   4. When it finishes, right-click the ZIP file and choose "Extract".
   5. IMPORTANT: after extracting, open public_html and check that you can see
      index.html, about.html, and the folders  admin, data, uploads, css, js, assets
      directly inside it. If they are inside another folder, select everything in that
      folder, move it up into public_html, then delete the empty folder.
   6. Delete the ZIP file from public_html (it is no longer needed).

B) Using FTP (FileZilla)
   1. Unzip the file on your computer.
   2. Connect to your hosting with FileZilla.
   3. Open public_html on the right side, then drag ALL the unzipped files and folders in.

STEP 2 - MAKE SURE THE SAVING FOLDERS CAN BE WRITTEN TO
-------------------------------------------------------
In File Manager, right-click each of these folders, choose "Permissions"
(or "Change Permissions"), and set the number to 755:
      data      uploads      admin
If, later, saving shows a message about permissions, change them to 775 and try again.

STEP 3 - LOG IN AND CHANGE THE DEFAULT PASSWORD (DO THIS FIRST!)
----------------------------------------------------------------
   1. In your browser go to:  yourwebsite.com/admin
   2. Log in with   admin   and   Admin@123
   3. You will see an orange warning on the dashboard. Click "Change password"
      (or click "Password & Security" on the left menu).
   4. Fill in:  your current password (Admin@123), a new username (any you like),
      a new password (at least 8 characters - mix letters, numbers and a symbol),
      then type the new password again.
   5. Click "Save new password". Done - the warning disappears.
   6. Write the new login somewhere safe. Use it from now on.

HOW TO EDIT YOUR WEBSITE
------------------------
   - Use the menu on the left: Home Page, Portfolio Page, About Page,
     Services & Prices, Contact & Social, Logo & Footer, All Images, Color Theme.
   - Change anything, then click the purple "Save changes" button (top right,
     or press Ctrl+S). Your live website updates immediately.
   - The top bar shows "Unsaved changes" until you save.
   - To change a picture: click "Upload new image" next to it, pick a photo,
     then click "Save changes". Large photos are made smaller automatically.
   - To add or remove services / projects / testimonials: use the "+ Add" button
     at the bottom of the list, or the red X on an item. Use the arrows to reorder.
   - Color Theme: click a theme and the whole live website changes color at once.
   - If your browser still shows the old version, press Ctrl+F5 to refresh.

IF YOU FORGET YOUR PASSWORD
---------------------------
Re-upload the file  admin/auth.php  from this ZIP to your hosting's admin folder
(replace the old one). The login goes back to admin / Admin@123 - then change it again.

VERY IMPORTANT WHEN UPDATING FILES LATER
-----------------------------------------
Never overwrite or delete these, or you will lose your edits and pictures:
      the "data" folder     the "uploads" folder     admin/auth.php
Back up the "data" and "uploads" folders now and then (download them to your computer).

NOTES
-----
   - Videos: you can upload MP4/WEBM files for portfolio projects. Your hosting may
     limit big files. If an upload fails, upload a smaller video, or paste a YouTube-free
     direct link/file path into the video box.
   - The contact form on the website is still in demo mode (as in your original
     template): it shows "Message sent" but does not email you. To receive messages,
     open js/contact.js and paste a form-service address (e.g. Formspree) into FORM_ENDPOINT.
   - You can't use the Admin Panel by double-clicking the files on your computer - it
     needs your hosting (PHP) to save changes.
